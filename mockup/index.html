The economy

Points a merchant can trust

You just saw it above: game points post as pending and only a purchase releases them. A photographed table QR earns exactly nothing.

Play at the venue

A venue-tagged game finishes. Card points post as pending — visible to the diner, unpaid to anyone.

Buy anything that day

MyPerx already records the transaction. It pings Play and the pending points confirm instantly. On the venue's Wi-Fi? Confirmed on the spot.

Points land on the merchant's card

Delivered to the MyPerx wallet with idempotency keys and retries. Daily cap per customer ≈ one small purchase.

Redeem at the counter

A 6-digit code, validated by staff in person. No purchase by closing time? Pending points expire quietly.

The failure mode is marketing: "You have pending points at Meraki — any purchase today unlocks them." An attempted cheat becomes a foot-traffic prospect.
